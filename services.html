Our Methodology

A Structured Project Lifecycle

Havilah Image Development Ltd adopts a structured project management lifecycle that ensures projects are delivered on time, within budget, and to the highest quality standards. This cyclical approach allows for continuous monitoring, proactive risk management, and seamless coordination among all stakeholders.

Start Your Project
1

Initiation & Mobilization

Project brief, stakeholder alignment, team mobilization, site assessment and preliminary planning to get every project off to the right start.

2

Design Management & Permitting

Architectural concept, design coordination across all disciplines, regulatory submissions and approval management (LASBCA, PTPPL, LSC).

3

Procurement & Award

Tender documentation, bid analysis, contractor clarifications, negotiation and formal contract award to best-value suppliers.

4

Construction Management

Site mobilization, HSE plan, QA/QC regime, daily supervision, progress reporting and stakeholder coordination throughout the build.

5

Testing & Commissioning

Systematic testing of all systems and services, snag list management and defects resolution prior to handover.

6

Close-out & Handover

Final inspections, as-built documentation, warranties, maintenance manuals and formal handover to the client.
