Our Story

A Vision Built on Accountability & Excellence

Havilah Image Development Ltd is an architect-led project management practice with over 15 years of experience delivering residential, commercial, and institutional projects across Nigeria.

We coordinate all stakeholders — from client teams to consultants and contractors — to drive clarity, accountability, and progress at every phase of a project.

Our services are driven by seasoned professionals committed to meeting a market need for viable, safe, and profitable investment opportunities through uncompromised values: accountability, proficiency, integrity, consistency, and innovation.

Accountability

Full transparency on investor funds and project financials at every stage.

Proficiency

Expert execution across architecture, engineering, QS, and legal domains.

Integrity

Honest dealings with clients, investors, contractors and all stakeholders.

Innovation

Fresh approaches to design, project delivery and investment structuring.
